Compliance lives in documents, and point tools only see a slice.

Classification tools give you a code. Extraction tools give you fields. Regulatory tools give you an answer. None of them leave you with evidence a regulator, auditor or customer can check.

The rules depend on where, not just what

The same product can be compliant in one county and rejected in the next. Requirements change on federal, state and local clocks.

The evidence breaks when goods change form

Lots get split, blended and manufactured into components. The certificate stays on a PDF while the product moves on.

"We relied on the supplier" is no longer a defense

Enforcement now reaches buyers who should have seen the red flags. A documented screening trail has become a control in its own right.

One orchestrator, many specialists, one verifiable record.

Every business event goes to an orchestrator (ours, or your own). It decides which specialist agents need to run and only lets the transaction move when the evidence is complete or a named person has signed off on the gap.

  1. Step 1

    Receive the event

    A purchase order, receipt, work order, shipment or rule change arrives through API, ERP or file drop.

  2. Step 2

    Load the rulebook

    The orchestrator attaches the right profile: product, origin, destination and jurisdiction.

  3. Step 3

    Run the specialists

    Agents work in parallel. Each returns pass, fail or missing evidence, with sources cited.

  4. Step 4

    Release or route

    Clean files move on. Exceptions go to a named person with a drafted fix.

Agents recommend, people decide

Holds, supplier exits, filings and statements to authorities always pass a named human.

If it isn't recorded, it didn't happen

Every check writes to a tamper-evident folder, anchored on a public ledger and shareable as W3C Verifiable Credentials.

Same core, different rulebooks.

Anything that is certified at origin, moved across borders, transformed and inspected at the end has the same shape. These are the industries we are building for, each with real civil or criminal exposure for getting it wrong.

VeriPura Core identifies the rules for any product moving from any origin to any destination, by HS code, wherever those rules can be obtained. That covers government regulations and the certifications buyers require, such as organic or halal. The rules shown below are examples only.

How we train the core for your industry.

Most of the value comes from rules and retrieval. We fine-tune only the narrow, high-volume steps where it pays.

Rules and sources

Versioned rulebooks and the source documents behind them, so every answer cites the exact version it relied on.

Example: a jurisdiction profile per state, or duty rates per producer and review period.

Trained specialists

Small, fast models tuned on your documents for the steps that run thousands of times a day.

Example: reading grade stamps, test reports or certificates in your suppliers' formats.

Orchestration and judgment

A frontier model with tools handles routing and exceptions, where inputs are new and mistakes are costly.

Example: drafting a supplier request when a declaration is incomplete.

Measured, not promised

Every pilot starts with a labeled test set from your own documents. We report extraction accuracy per field, flag precision and recall, and false-hold rate. Each reviewer decision becomes training data, so accuracy improves with use.
