What we tune for cosmetics

Nine stages, tuned for cosmetics.

VeriPura Cosmetics™ runs the same find, check and prove loop as every industry solution built on VeriPura Core™. For cosmetics, the agents are tuned on ingredient and color additive rules, labeling and claims requirements, product registration and notification, and the bodies that certify cosmetics, such as cruelty-free, halal and organic certifiers. At the check stage, agents do not rely on the documents alone: they take in inputs from third-party validators and cross-check claims against independent data sources, so each finding rests on an outside source of truth, not only on what the paperwork says.

What we check.

Tuned for cosmetics, VeriPura Core can check any cosmetic product moving from any origin to any destination, wherever the rules can be obtained. The rules below are examples only.

For example the annexes to EU Regulation 1223/2009, FDA color additive rules and China's cosmetic ingredient inventory.

Labeling and claims

For example EU labeling and claims rules, and the line between cosmetic and drug claims in the US.

Registration and notification

For example US facility registration and product listing, the EU CPNP and the UK SCPN.

Buyer-required certifications

For example cruelty-free, halal, vegan and organic, which differ by country and certifier.

Questions.

What is VeriPura Cosmetics™?

VeriPura Core tuned for cosmetics. It checks formulas, labels, claims, registrations and certificates against the requirements of any country of origin and any country of destination before a shipment departs.

How does it relate to VeriPura Core™?

VeriPura Cosmetics™ is the industry solution built on VeriPura Core™ tuned for cosmetics: the same orchestrated agent engine, with agents trained on cosmetics rules, documents and certifiers. Other industries, such as food, supplements and pharmaceuticals, have their own tuned editions.

Is VeriPura Core™ patent-pending?

Yes. The orchestration and verification approach is covered under VeriPura's USPTO filing, U.S. provisional patent application 64/088,697. The filing secures our date for later non-provisional and foreign patent filings.

Can our IT team use it directly?

Yes, through our hosted API. Talk to an engineer to discuss what your team needs.

Which documents does it check?

Formulas and ingredient lists, label artwork, product information files, safety assessments, certificates of analysis, registration and notification records, and the other documents the destination market requires.

Which frameworks does it cover?

Any framework for any country of origin and any country of destination, wherever the rules can be obtained, plus certifications your buyers require. Examples include EU Regulation 1223/2009, the US Modernization of Cosmetics Regulation Act and FDA color additive rules, UK cosmetics regulations and China's cosmetics registration rules.

What happens when an ingredient is banned?

The agents check each formula against the current rules for the destination. When a rule changes, the products it affects can be checked again before the next shipment.

What does a pilot look like?

It starts with one real shipment. We show every rule that applied, what was missing and any red flags. Your team makes the decision. If it's useful, a paid pilot follows in shadow mode, with the fee credited toward a contract.
