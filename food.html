What we tuned for food

Nine stages, tuned for food.

VeriPura Food™ runs the same find, check and prove loop as every industry solution built on VeriPura Core™. For food, the 50+ specialist agents are trained on food import and food safety rules, health and phytosanitary certificates, and the bodies that certify food, such as halal and organic certifiers. At the check stage, agents do not rely on the documents alone: they take in inputs from third-party validators and cross-check claims against independent data sources, so each finding rests on an outside source of truth, not only on what the paperwork says.

Questions.

What is VeriPura Food™?

VeriPura Core tuned for food. It checks phytosanitary and health certificates, certificates of origin and analysis, and regulatory declarations against the requirements of any country of origin and any country of destination before a container departs.

How does it relate to VeriPura Core™?

VeriPura Food™ is the industry solution built on VeriPura Core™ tuned for food: the same orchestrated agent engine, with agents trained on food rules, documents and certifiers. Food is where the engine was built and tested first. Other industries, such as cosmetics and pharmaceuticals, have their own tuned editions.

Is VeriPura Core™ patent-pending?

Yes. The orchestration and verification approach is covered under VeriPura's USPTO filing, U.S. provisional patent application 64/088,697. The filing secures our date for later non-provisional and foreign patent filings.

Can our IT team use it directly?

Yes, through our hosted API. Talk to an engineer to discuss what your team needs.

Which documents does it check?

Phytosanitary certificates, health certificates, certificates of origin, certificates of analysis, common entry documents, FDA Prior Notice submissions, fumigation certificates and the other documents the destination market requires.

Which frameworks does it cover?

Any framework for any country of origin and any country of destination, wherever the rules can be obtained, plus buyer-required certifications such as halal and organic. Examples include EU Regulation 2017/625, TRACES NT, FDA FSMA, FSMA 204 traceability, EUDR and UK FRCR.

How does it prevent FDA automatic detentions?

Detentions are usually triggered by documentation errors such as a wrong product code in Prior Notice, a missing field or an authority mismatch. VeriPura checks each relevant field before departure and flags issues while they can still be fixed.

How does it help with deforestation rules?

For beef, soy, cocoa, coffee and palm oil, VeriPura checks due diligence records such as geolocation data and deforestation statements as part of the pre-shipment check, and flags gaps before departure.

What happens when VeriPura cannot check something?

It says so. A missing document is a finding. A register that is offline shows as Not checked, never as a match, and every result shows the date of the data behind it. Your team decides what to do next.

What does a pilot look like?

It starts with one real consignment. We show every rule that applied, what was missing and any red flags. Your team makes the decision. If it's useful, a paid pilot follows in shadow mode, with the fee credited toward a contract.
