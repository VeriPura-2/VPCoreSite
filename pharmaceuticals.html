Questions.

What is VeriPura Pharma™?

VeriPura Core tuned for pharmaceuticals. It checks licences, GMP and GDP certificates, certificates of analysis, batch records and cold chain data against the requirements of any country of origin and any country of destination before a shipment departs.

How does it relate to VeriPura Core™?

VeriPura Pharma™ is the edition of VeriPura Core™ tuned for pharmaceuticals: the same orchestrated agent engine, with agents trained on pharmaceuticals rules, documents and certifiers. Other industries, such as food, cosmetics and supplements, have their own tuned editions.

Can our IT team use it directly?

Yes. Every pharma-tuned agent can be called over API or MCP from your own systems, one at a time or as the full pipeline. Request sandbox access to try it.

Which documents does it check?

Certificates of analysis, GMP and GDP certificates, written confirmations, import licences, marketing authorisations, batch records, temperature logs, controlled substance permits and the other documents the destination market requires.

Which frameworks does it cover?

Any framework for any country of origin and any country of destination, wherever the rules can be obtained. Examples include EU Directive 2001/83/EC and the Falsified Medicines Directive, EU GMP and GDP, the US Drug Supply Chain Security Act and FDA import requirements, and WHO guidelines.

How does it help against falsified products?

Agents compare each document with the others and with independent sources, such as public registers of manufacturing sites, and flag mismatches that could point to a substandard or falsified product before the shipment moves.

Does it replace our qualified person or regulatory team?

No. It prepares the evidence and flags issues. Your qualified people make the decisions.

What does a pilot look like?

It starts with a free compliance check on one real shipment. If that shows value, a 12-week paid pilot follows in shadow mode, with the fee credited toward a contract.
