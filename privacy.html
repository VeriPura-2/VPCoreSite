Last updated: 7 October 2026

This notice explains how VeriPura handles personal data collected through veripura.com and its industry sites, such as food.veripura.com: the contact form, the link to book a call, and your visit to the site itself. It is written to meet the UK GDPR and the EU General Data Protection Regulation.

Who we are

[Company legal name], [registered address], trading as VeriPura ("VeriPura", "we", "us"), is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact us about privacy at [privacy email address].

What we collect

  • Contact form. Your name, company, work email, the type of engagement you are interested in (for example a pilot, a license or sandbox access), your industry, and your message.
  • Booking a call. If you book through our Google booking page, Google processes your name, email address, the time you choose and any answers you give, and shares them with us.
  • Visiting the site. Our hosting provider and font provider receive technical data such as your IP address and browser type when the page loads.
  • Display preference. If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your own browser. It is not sent to us.

Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To reply to your request, arrange a call, and prepare a compliance check, pilot or proposal you asked about. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a contract, or our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.
  • To keep the site working and secure. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating the site.

We do not sell your personal data, we do not use it to train AI models, and we do not add you to marketing emails unless you ask us to.

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Formspree, which delivers contact form submissions to us by email.
  • Google, for our booking page, calendar and email.
  • Our hosting provider, [hosting provider], which serves the site.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the typefaces used on the site.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it.
